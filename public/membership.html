Common Questions

Can I cancel my membership anytime?

Basic and Standard plans can be cancelled at the end of their term. Premium annual plans include a 30-day money-back guarantee.

Is there a joining fee?

No joining fee! The price you see is all you pay. We believe in complete transparency.

Can I freeze my membership?

Standard and Premium members can freeze their membership for up to 30 days per year (e.g., for travel or injury).

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards, and net banking.
